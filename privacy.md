# Tadah — Privacy Policy

Effective date: Nov 5, 2026

**The short version: Tadah does not collect any of your data.** Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no cloud sync run by the developer.

Tadah is published by Milana Vladimirova, based in Switzerland ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app stores on your device and how you stay in control of it. It is written with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in mind.

## What the app stores, and where

Tadah stores only what you create in it, and only on your device:

- Your tasks
- Your projects and categories
- Your preferences: theme, reminder settings and daily goal

This data lives in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. I have no access to it, and it is never sent to me or to anyone else.

If you use Apple's own iCloud Backup or computer backups, your device backup may include Tadah's data. Those backups are managed by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by me.

## Automatic local backups

To protect you against data loss, Tadah regularly saves backup copies of your data as JSON files. These copies are stored in the app's private storage on your device (Application Support). They never leave your device.

Only a few recent copies are kept: older ones are deleted automatically as new ones are created.

## Export and import

You can export a backup file of your data, and import one back into the app. This only happens when you choose to do it.

When you export, you decide where the file goes through the iOS share sheet (for example Files, AirDrop or email). From then on, the file is handled by the service you chose and its own privacy policy. Tadah does not send it anywhere on its own.

## Notifications

Tadah can send you an optional daily reminder. It is off until you turn it on and grant permission.

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled on your device and do not go through any server. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

## What Tadah does not do

- No account or sign-in
- No servers: your data is never transferred to me or to any server I operate
- No analytics, crash-tracking or measurement SDKs
- No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties
- No access to your camera, photos, contacts, location, microphone or health data

## Your rights and control

Because your data never reaches me, I cannot view, correct or delete it for you, but you can do all of this yourself:

- **Access:** see all your data directly in the app, or export it as a file.
- **Correction:** edit any task, project or setting in the app.
- **Deletion:** delete items in the app. Uninstalling Tadah removes all its data from your device, including the automatic backups.

Copies you exported yourself, and Apple device backups, are not removed by uninstalling. You can delete them where you stored them.

## Children

Tadah collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will publish the new version at this address and update the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
